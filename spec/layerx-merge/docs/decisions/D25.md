# D25: Exchange withdrawMargin

Status: decided

## Context
The exchange keeper's WithdrawMargin records a withdrawal intent (modules/layerxexchange/keeper/intents.go:82) that no kernel activity consumes. The kernel releases margin only through its perps margin release (src/modules/perps/lx_perps_position.c:95), and margin accounts are one per DID per market (src/modules/perps/lx_perps_dispatch.c:132).

## Options considered
| Option | Consequence |
|---|---|
| A: keep withdrawMargin as a bank release from the margin address to the main address with a free-margin check | The method stays available; one new vector |
| B: retire it; margin leaves only through close, fills, liquidation and ADL | Clients lose the method |

## Decision
Option A is decided: withdrawMargin is an immediate bank release from the caller DID's margin sub-address for a market to its main address, gated by a free-margin check, mirroring the kernel margin release.

## Consequences
- WithdrawMargin is rewritten from an intent record to a bank release (modules/layerxexchange/keeper/intents.go:82).
- The release mirrors the kernel margin release and its free-margin rule (src/modules/perps/lx_perps_position.c:95).
- The method takes a market argument, since the margin account is one per DID per market (src/modules/perps/lx_perps_dispatch.c:132).

## Affects
- Addresses: 0x1015 layerxexchange
- Modules: layerxexchange, bank
