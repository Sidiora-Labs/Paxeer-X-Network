# D5: Kernel balances without a 0x1004 binding at activation

Status: not applicable

## Context
The question covered kernel balances held by a DID with no 0x1004 binding when the kernel domain folds into the chain. The binding is held by the evm module binding keeper (modules/evm/keeper/layerx_binding.go:18).

## Options considered
| Option | Consequence |
|---|---|
| A: a claim record payable once the DID binds, through a small claim method | One small method kept |
| B: a treasury hold with manual release | No new method; every release is manual |

## Decision
Not applicable: activation is a bring-up at a height; no account-state migration is required. The upgrade handler carries no claim record, no claim method and no treasury hold.

## Consequences
- No balance migration step is part of the plan.
- The 0x1004 binding keeper is unchanged by this decision (modules/evm/keeper/layerx_binding.go:18).

## Affects
- Addresses: 0x1004 addr
- Modules: upgrade handler
