# D4: Custody claims and guarantor bonds at the activation height

Status: not applicable

## Context
The question covered settlement of open custody claims and guarantor bonds when the kernel domain folds into the chain. The custody keeper carries a cancel path for claims (modules/layerxcustody/keeper/claims.go:455).

## Options considered
| Option | Consequence |
|---|---|
| A: settle in the upgrade handler through CancelClaim, then import bonds | One upgrade step and one golden |
| B: keep custody and anchor exit paths live for a drain window, then delete | Two exit paths live during the window |
| C: pay every queued claim early | Payouts outside normal claim timing |

## Decision
Not applicable: activation is a bring-up at a height; no account-state migration is required. No claim settlement, drain window or early payout is part of this feature. The upgrade handler returns every posted guarantor bond to its poster.

## Consequences
- No claim-settlement golden is required.
- The upgrade handler step returns posted guarantor bonds, with a focused test.
- The custody and anchor retirement tasks proceed without a settlement step (modules/layerxcustody/keeper/claims.go:455).

## Affects
- Addresses: 0x1014 layerxanchor
- Modules: layerxcustody, layerxanchor, upgrade handler
