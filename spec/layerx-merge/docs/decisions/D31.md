# D31: ABI form of computeClearing and scoreBatch

Status: decided

## Context
clearing at 0x0902 and pofq at 0x0904 are routed by the gateway (human/wallet/gateway/src/precompiles.ts:19-21). The earlier implementation defined computeClearing and scoreBatch in two forms: a struct form in the Solidity interfaces (selectors 0x8397e9f8, 0x1b1fba48) and a parallel-array form (selectors 0xb6a4c9ae, 0xc6f7591b). The matching engine caller encodes the struct form.

## Options considered
| Option | Consequence |
|---|---|
| A: struct ABI only | One selector per method; matches the caller |
| B: struct ABI plus the parallel-array selectors | Two selectors per method |

## Decision
Option A is decided: computeClearing and scoreBatch are served in the struct ABI form only, with one selector per method.

## Consequences
- 0x0902 serves computeClearing at 0x8397e9f8 and 0x0904 serves scoreBatch at 0x1b1fba48 (human/wallet/gateway/src/precompiles.ts:19-21).
- The parallel-array selectors are not served.

## Affects
- Addresses: 0x0902 clearing, 0x0904 pofq
- Modules: layerxagent precompiles
