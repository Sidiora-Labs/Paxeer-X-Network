# Retired components and their callers

When the LayerX kernel domain is folded into the Paxeer X Network, 47 components that exist only because there are two domains are retired at activation or re-pointed. Activation is a bring-up at the activation height; no account-state migration is required. The upgrade handler returns posted guarantor bonds only (ADR D4).

Retired precompile addresses keep a registered executor that returns `Error(string)` "retired", gated per method by height on the pattern of `IsUpgradeActiveAtHeight` (modules/layerxcustody/keeper/keeper.go:42). The executors stay registered in precompiles/setup.go:81-83, so a call to a retired address returns the retired error and never succeeds empty.

Waves: 0 sequencer, fee policy and gas station; 1 compatibility shim, maps, archive node and AI evaluator service; 2 ledger, pay and auth constructs; 3 layerxprograms, origin-2 xweb and exchange engine; 4 AI guests, worker, keeper and the 402LXP sidecar draw; 5 caller re-pointing; 6 activation upgrade handler; 7 retirement of two-domain components.
